USE CASES

When conditions change, governance responds.

A person changes roles. A qualification expires. A mission begins. A device becomes compromised. An organization crosses a trust boundary. The underlying pattern remains consistent.

THE PATTERN

Change → Governance → Result

CRU becomes especially useful when something changes. Identity can persist while governance determines what changing state, evidence, and context mean now.
