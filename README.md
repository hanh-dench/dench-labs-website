## CRU

**CRU** stands for **Canonical Representation Unit**.

A CRU is a portable, cryptographically verifiable digital representation that provides a canonical representation of an entity, authority, capability, governance artifact, or other digitally representable subject.

Unlike conventional digital representations that depend upon centralized repositories, databases, identity providers, blockchain networks, or other persistent infrastructure, a CRU is intrinsically independent of any particular storage technology or communication mechanism. The authenticity and integrity of a CRU are established through cryptographic verification rather than by the location from which the representation is obtained.

CRU(S) refers to one or more Canonical Representation Units.
